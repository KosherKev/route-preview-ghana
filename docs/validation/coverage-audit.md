# Real-coverage audit (Phase 3 follow-up D)

Generated: 2026-11-20T20:31:34.764Z. Generated by `npm run audit:coverage` (backend); not typed by hand. This report states measurements only. It gives no go/no-go verdict and applies no age filter or threshold change.

## Method

1. Route geometry: each of the 25 validated route pairs is routed with Valhalla under both costings (motor_scooter and auto), giving up to 50 route polylines.
2. Corridor: a point counts as inside the corridor when it is within 30 m of any of those polylines. To limit requests, the polylines are covered by fixed 0.002 degree cells (about 223 m); 757 cells were requested. Images the API returned outside the 30 m corridor were discarded before storage.
3. Ingestion: Mapillary Graph API v4 /images, metadata fields only (the same field list the Phase 2 ingester requests), one bbox request per cell, throttled to one request start per 500 ms, Retry-After honoured by the Phase 2 client (retry cap 5 per request). Rows are upserted into the local PostGIS `frames` table by the Phase 2 store (idempotent on image_id). No image file, thumbnail or pixel data is requested, downloaded or stored.
4. Classification: each route is classified twice from the stored frames under the current rules (two-tier gap rule: RIFE below 15 m, crossfade bridge 15 to 30 m inclusive, at most 2 bridges per segment, bridging only within one sequence_id; frame filters: lateral tolerance 15 m, at most 3 m left of centreline, heading within 25 degrees of the road, quality_score at least 0.5): over the WHOLE route, and over the final approach (the last 500 m), which is what the Phase 3 validation classified. The two are reported separately.
5. Shares are length-weighted: street share = STREET_VIDEO metres divided by route metres, totals are summed metres over summed metres (not an average of per-route percentages).

## Limitations

- Mapillary image metadata only. No image, thumbnail or pixel data was fetched, so nothing here says whether a frame is sharp, usable, correctly exposed, free of obstruction, or shows what the metadata claims.
- quality_score is Mapillary's own number; it is used as reported. There is no independent image-quality check.
- Thresholds (60 m minimum run, 15 m RIFE ceiling, 30 m bridge ceiling, 2 bridges per segment, 25 degree heading tolerance, 0.5 quality, 15 m lateral) are the Phase 3 defaults and are untuned against this data.
- A 'street' share is a geometric statement about usable frame spacing along a Valhalla route, not a statement that a rider-facing preview would look acceptable.
- Capture-age buckets describe the frames inside STREET_VIDEO segments. Old imagery may show a road that has since changed; no age filter is applied and none is recommended or rejected here.
- Only a 30 m corridor around the 25 validated pairs (both costings) was ingested. Coverage elsewhere in Accra is not measured, and 25 pairs are a sample of trips, not of the road network.
- Last-500 m approach figures use the existing approach window; very short routes use the whole route as the approach.
- Frames are matched with one sequence per edge (the Phase 3 rule); a corridor with several overlapping sequences is reduced to one, so frame counts are lower than raw image counts.
- Where a route-costing pair failed to route, it contributes nothing to totals (see the Routes column of the totals table).
- The existing docs/validation/route-validation.md is the earlier no-imagery run (empty frames table) and is left unchanged; its 'not measured' statement predates this audit.
